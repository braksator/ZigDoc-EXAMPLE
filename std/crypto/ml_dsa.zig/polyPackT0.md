# polyPackT0

Pack polynomial with coefficients in (-2^(D-1), 2^(D-1)] (T0) into bytes.
Packs 13 bits per coefficient: 8 coefficients into 13 bytes.
Assumes coefficients are not normalized, but in (q-2^(D-1), q+2^(D-1)].
