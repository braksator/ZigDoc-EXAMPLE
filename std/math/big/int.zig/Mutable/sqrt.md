# sqrt

r = ⌊√a⌋

r may alias a.

Asserts that `r` has enough limbs to store the result. Upper bound is
`(a.limbs.len - 1) / 2 + 1`.

`limbs_buffer` is used for temporary storage.
The amount required is given by `calcSqrtLimbsBufferLen`.
