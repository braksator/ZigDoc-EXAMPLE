# expm1

Returns e raised to the power of x, minus 1 (e^x - 1). This is more accurate than exp(e, x) - 1
when x is near 0.

Special Cases:
 - expm1(+inf) = +inf
 - expm1(-inf) = -1
 - expm1(nan)  = nan
