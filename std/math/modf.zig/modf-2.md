# modf

Returns the integer and fractional floating-point numbers that sum to x. The sign of each
result is the same as the sign of x.
In comptime, may be used with comptime_float

Special Cases:
 - modf(+-inf) = +-inf, nan
 - modf(nan)   = nan, nan
