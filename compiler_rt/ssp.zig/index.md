# ssp.zig


Small Zig reimplementation of gcc's libssp.

This library implements most of the builtins required by the stack smashing
protection as implemented by gcc&clang.
Missing exports:
- __gets_chk
- __mempcpy_chk
- __snprintf_chk
- __sprintf_chk
- __stpcpy_chk
- __vsnprintf_chk
- __vsprintf_chk
